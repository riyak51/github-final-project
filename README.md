## Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator calculates the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specified period at a given annual interest rate.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- Principal: The initial amount of money.
- Rate: The annual interest rate in percentage.
- Time: The time period in years.

## Example

- Principal: 1000
- Rate: 5%
- Time: 2 years

Calculation:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

Result: The simple interest is 100.
